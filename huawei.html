Inside Petal Search:
My Role and Contribution

Petal Search is Huawei’s search engine for apps, news, sports, shopping and local services, launched internationally in 2020. My role began with keyword search and expanded into the end-to-end planning and execution of campaigns in WiseOper.

Key Milestones

Petal Search ↗AppGallery ↗
  1. May 2020

    Huawei first introduced Petal Search (originally known as Huawei Search) for international markets to help users find third-party apps and APK files following the loss of Google Mobile Services (GMS).

  2. June 2020

    Petal Search was listed on Huawei’s AppGallery.

  3. September 2020

    At the Huawei Developer Conference (HDC 2020) from September 10 to 12, Petal Search was formally showcased as a broader Google Search competitor.

  4. October 2020

    During the Huawei Mate 40 Series Global Online Launch Event on October 22, 2020, Huawei officially released Petal Search as a full-fledged global search engine.

  5. Tokyo 2020 Olympics

    Led the Olympics campaign by refreshing the Sports section with event coverage and driving traffic to it through in-app shortcuts, banners, sidebars and pop-up notifications.

  6. Expo 2020

    Expo 2020 feature launched in the Petal Search app.

  7. 15–31 December 2021

    Petal Search Shopping Festival across the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Egypt and South Africa, with vouchers from noon, Jumia, Wego and other partners.

  8. New Year 2022

    New Year 2022 campaign launched across the APAC region, spanning shopping and Nearby.

  9. Ramadan

    Ramadan Hub launched in the Middle East and Africa.

08

Mobile and Web Testing

Before every launch, I carried out end-to-end quality assurance across the Petal Search app and web views to ensure each campaign rendered and performed consistently for every user.

  • Cross-device validation

    Tested app and web views across multiple screen sizes, resolutions and Huawei devices to confirm responsive layouts and correct asset rendering.

  • Link and navigation checks

    Verified that every doodle, banner and call to action routed to the correct landing page, with deep links and redirects resolving as intended.

  • Localisation review

    Reviewed copy, imagery, currency and date formats for each market across APAC, MENA and LATAM.

  • Tracking verification

    Confirmed analytics tags and campaign parameters fired correctly so that performance data was accurate from launch.

  • Pre-launch sign-off

    Logged and resolved issues with stakeholders, then completed final checks in staging before campaigns went live.

  • Post-launch monitoring

    Monitored live campaigns for display and performance issues and applied fixes promptly.

09

Voice and Speech Recognition Testing

I conducted functional and user acceptance testing (UAT) of the voice search feature, validating that voice commands were accurately recognised, transcribed and converted into relevant search results.

  • Speech-to-text accuracy

    Verified transcription accuracy across a range of voice queries, phrasing and speaking speeds.

  • Accent and language coverage

    Tested recognition across regional accents and supported languages to ensure consistent results in every market.

  • Intent and query matching

    Confirmed that spoken commands mapped to the correct search intent, results and in-app destinations.

  • Environment and edge cases

    Assessed performance in noisy environments, with partial or interrupted commands, and when microphone permissions were denied.

  • Response time and UI states

    Checked listening, processing and error states for responsiveness and clear user feedback.

  • Defect reporting

    Documented issues with reproducible steps and retested fixes before release.
